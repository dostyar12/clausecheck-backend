## Description (paste-ready)
> ClauseCheck is a browser extension that translates legalese into plain English. Highlight any legal text — a Terms of Service, a rental lease, a client contract — right-click, and in about five seconds you get:
>
> - A plain-English summary of what the clause actually means
> - The specific red flags you should worry about
> - A risk score: Low / Medium / High / Critical
>
> Built for freelancers signing client contracts, renters reading leases, and anyone who's one "I Agree" click away from agreeing to something they don't understand.
>
> No accounts. No tracking. Text is processed and discarded immediately. One-time payment — no subscription.
>
> Try it free on the website: paste any clause and get an explanation with no sign-up.
